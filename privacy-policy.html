Privacy Policy

Gecko Record Hub · Effective November 14, 2026

The short version

Gecko Record Hub keeps your records on your iPhone. We don't collect, receive, sell or share any of your data, and the app contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

What the app stores

The animals, enclosures, meals, sheds, weigh-ins, readings, notes, events, health records, breeding and sales records, and photos that you enter. They're stored only in the app's storage on your device, and in your device backup (iCloud Backup or your computer) if you use one.

Photos and camera

The app only sees photos you choose or take inside it. They're copied into the app's storage. It doesn't read the rest of your photo library.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved.

Widgets

Home and lock screen widgets read a summary that the app saves in storage shared only between the app and its widgets on your device.

Exporting and deleting your data

Settings › Export records saves every record as a CSV file you can share. Deleting the app deletes all its data from your device. Device backups follow your backup settings.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
