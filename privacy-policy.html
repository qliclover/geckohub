Privacy Policy

Gecko Record Hub · Effective November 15, 2026 (updated for the care guide)

The short version

Gecko Record Hub keeps your records on your iPhone. If you choose to sign in, your records and photos sync to your account so your other devices get them, and we keep the email address you sign in with. Only you can read your synced records, unless you choose to share an enclosure with someone. We don't sell or share your data, and the app contains no ads, analytics or tracking. The care guide loads public species information from Wikipedia and iNaturalist; none of your records are sent.

What the app stores

The animals, enclosures, meals, sheds, weigh-ins, readings, notes, events, health records, breeding and sales records, and photos that you enter. They're stored only in the app's storage on your device, and in your device backup (iCloud Backup or your computer) if you use one.

Your account (optional)

You can use the app without an account. If you sign in, you can use Sign in with Apple or an email address. We store that email address and an account ID so you can sign in again. With Sign in with Apple, you can choose to hide your email from us.

Sign-in codes are sent by email. Accounts are hosted by Supabase (supabase.com), and emails are sent through our email provider. They process this information only to run sign-in for us.

While you're signed in, your records (animals, enclosures, meals, sheds, weigh-ins, readings, notes, events, health, breeding and sales records) and their photos are stored in your account so they sync between your devices. They're stored with Supabase, protected so only your account, and people you invite to shared care, can read them. Without an account, nothing is sent to us.

Shared care (optional)

If you invite someone to shared care, the enclosure you choose (or all your reptiles, if you choose that), its animals, their records and photos become visible to that person's account, and they can add records if you allow it. They see the name you enter for the invite; you see the name they enter when they join. You can remove someone, and they can leave, at any time; their copy of your records is then removed from their device. Invites are one-time codes that expire after 7 days.

Photos and camera

The app only sees photos you choose or take inside it. They're copied into the app's storage. It doesn't read the rest of your photo library.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved.

Widgets

Home and lock screen widgets read a summary that the app saves in storage shared only between the app and its widgets on your device.

Care guide

When you open a species in the care guide, the app fetches that species' public summary from Wikipedia (wikipedia.org) and a photo from iNaturalist (inaturalist.org), and saves them on your device so the guide works offline. These requests contain only the species name and your app language. Like any website, those services see your device's IP address when it connects. No records, photos or account details are sent. Care sheet links open in your browser, under that website's own privacy policy.

Exporting and deleting your data

Settings › Account › Export all records saves every record and photo in one file you can share. Settings › Account › Delete account permanently deletes your account and every synced record and photo from our server, and erases everything the app stores on your device. Signing out removes your records from that device; they stay in your account and come back when you sign in again. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your device. Device backups follow your backup settings.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
