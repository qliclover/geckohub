Common questions

Where are my records? On your iPhone, inside the app. They're included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup.

How do I move records to a new phone? Restore the new phone from a backup of the old one. You can also use Settings › Export records to keep a CSV copy.

I'm not getting reminders. Check that notifications for Gecko Hub are on in the iPhone Settings app, and look at the quiet hours under Settings › Reminders in the app.

Is this veterinary advice? No. Care ranges are common keeper guidance. Ask a reptile vet about your animal's health.
